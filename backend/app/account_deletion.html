What happens to your data

Your active trips will be cancelled. Your ORIX profile, contact details, uploaded avatar, ride requests, preferences, ratings and blocking records will be removed along with your ORIX sign-in identity.

Your messages and identifying details in shared groups, reports and support conversations will be removed or anonymized. Other members' unrelated accounts and trip records will remain.

Deletion runs in the background and may take longer if a service is unavailable. Submitting a request does not mean cleanup has already finished. This does not delete your Google account.
