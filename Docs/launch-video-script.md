# ORIX launch film — “Mandhi Mission”

Draft 1 · 2026-11-04 · Awaiting owner script approval.

## Film contract

A colorful animated Kerala comic about a student whose mandhi plan almost loses
to his travel budget. ORIX helps him find company for the same journey. The food
is the hook; coordinating travel is the product; eating together is the payoff.

Target approximately 120 seconds for this feature-rich launch cut. This is a
proposed editorial length, not a locked music edit. The supplied track measures
240.396 seconds. Use an approved excerpt/edit of that track, not unrelated music.
ORIX enters around second 29, leaving most of the film for the product and payoff.

**Only audio:** the supplied ORIX theme song. No character voices, voiceover,
foley, additional sound effects, notification pings or added percussion. Preserve
the provided song's own vocal/instrumental content. Speech and thoughts exist as
Manglish comic bubbles. Cut to musical accents and phrasing, not lyric meaning.

**Fully animated:** performance, moving limbs, dance steps, prop handling, facial
takes and secondary movement. This is not a slideshow or a succession of still
illustrations with zooms. The camera supports the acting. Bubbles settle for
reading while characters retain subtle movement. No lip-sync to invented speech.

All timing below is provisional. Do not say the film is synchronized until the
actual animation and rendered audio have been reviewed together.

## Script — text in bold is intended for the picture

### 1. College is over — 0–3 s

Open in motion, at SJCET Palai. Our student dance-walks from the main-block
approach: heel-toe step, shoulder groove, backpack swing. Use the recognizable
clock tower, cream structural frames, red panels and pitched tiled roofs.
An establishing shot becomes a moving close-up of his bouncing backpack.

No opening paragraph or logo-only hold. His grin and body language establish the
vibe; the campus sign provides the location.

### 2. A very important financial plan — 3–7 s

He checks his phone without losing the step. A clean, generic balance read shows
**₹250**. He raises an eyebrow, then smiles.

Thought: **“Innu mandhi kazhikkaam!”**

Keep this phone view separate from ORIX; ORIX is not a bank or payment app.

### 3. Mandhi multiverse — 7–10 s

His thought bubble expands into a saturated food fantasy. A mandhi platter spins,
a cooked chicken drumstick does a heel-kick, rice grains pop in a choreographed
wave and steam curls into a dancing silhouette. He joins their pose for a beat.
Show **₹180** as a small food-price tag in this fantasy so the budget has a cause.

Use expressive food characters with coherent silhouettes, not photoreal food
distortion or live chickens being harmed. End on the circular platter; match-cut
that circle to an auto wheel at the nearby stand.

### 4. Fare enters the chat — 10–17 s

He steps up to a parked auto. The driver taps the steering wheel and bobs his head
to the same song. The engine and horn are silent in the soundtrack.

Boy: **“Chetta, Bharananganam vare ethra?”**

Driver: **“₹100.”**

The boy's shoulders sink, but one foot keeps tapping. His thought becomes a tiny
comic calculation: **₹250 − ₹100 = ₹150**. The dancing mandhi shrinks behind it.

Thought: **“Appo mandhi...?”**

### 5. Auto chettan understands — 17–26 s

Driver leans forward, friendly, not mocking.

Driver: **“Enthu patti, bro?”**

Boy: **“Auto kooli koduthaal mandhikku thikayilla!”**

The driver points to the empty passenger space, then makes a sharing gesture.

Driver: **“Koottukaare vilikku. Auto share cheythaal mathi!”**

Each bubble arrives in speaker order, never simultaneously. The joke is his
over-serious food budget, not loneliness or poverty.

### 6. Enter ORIX — 26–33 s

Boy gives a small embarrassed shrug, still doing the tiniest shoulder groove.

Boy: **“Athinu enikku ivide friends illallo...”**

A nearby student overhears, steps into the panel and turns her phone toward him.

Student: **“Chetta, ORIX use cheyyu!”**

The actual ORIX home screen fills her phone. Its route control carries us into
the next scene. Her different destination will explain why she recommends the
app rather than simply becoming his shared-auto companion herself.

### 7. The route finds its people — 33–48 s

Show the real journey flow: starting point near SJCET, destination Bharananganam,
departure date/time window and the actual preference controls. One action at a
time; magnify the control being used without inventing a different screen.

Student's guiding bubble: **“Route-um time-um kodukku.”**

Boy's thought: **“Ithe vazhikku vere aarenkilum undo?”**

Show the actual searching state. The comic returns briefly to the student keeping
time with his shoe; elsewhere, another traveller checks a phone and picks up a bag.
Then return to the real group state. Do not display an instant-match stopwatch.

Passenger journey creation is route/preferences based. Do not invent three
Auto/Partner/Lift selection buttons. In the source, compatible requests form a
pool and the assigned journey can become an auto group or include a lift.

### 8. Meet before you move — 48–62 s

He opens the group and checks member profiles. Names/photos and occupation are
the real captured fields. Another traveller mirrors his small wave in a separate
comic panel; the panels meet only after the product has established the group.

Thought: **“Aaraanu koode? Nokki confirm cheyyaam.”**

In group chat, one message is sent and the other arrives:

**“Njaan auto standil undu.”**

**“Njaan ippo ethum.”**

Show the actual pickup/readiness controls and confirmation state. Animate the
characters making a plan, rather than having screenshots float in empty space.

### 9. Your comfort matters — 62–81 s

Three connected mini-scenes, carried by the same phone rather than unrelated
feature slides:

1. The female student who introduced ORIX sets her own women-only preference for
   her different journey. Caption: **“Ente comfort. Ente choice.”**
2. A group is reviewed and passed on; the existing journey returns to searching.
   Caption: **“Set aayille? Vere group nokkaam.”**
3. Actual block/report controls are shown without inventing an incident or
   displaying a real person's private report. Caption:
   **“Prashnam undo? Block cheyyaam. Report cheyyaam.”**

Keep this section calm and respectful, with clear gestures and readable controls.
The male lead never enables women-only matching. Rejection does not create a new
journey. No tick or shield is used to suggest identity/safety checks the UI does
not actually provide.

### 10. More than this one auto — 81–89 s

The route line extends into two moving comic scenes, then returns to our auto:

- Two travellers walking together toward their agreed pickup/transport point.
  Caption: **“Koode yaathra cheyyaam.”**
- A driver preparing a specific lift offer with route, time and seats, through
  the actual offer flow. Caption: **“Seat undo? Lift offer cheyyaam.”**

Do not turn the student's occupation into driver permissions. No recurring-lift
or guaranteed-seat claim. Keep the main character's trip a shared auto.

### 11. Mandhi mission restored — 89–102 s

Our matched traveller arrives, waves and joins the boy at the auto. The driver
holds up two fingers and points to the two passengers.

Driver: **“Randu perum ₹50 vechu thannaal mathi.”**

The boy's thought calculation becomes **₹250 − ₹50 = ₹200**. His mandhi fantasy
returns in full color. He beams at the other traveller.

Boy: **“Bro... mandhi?”**

Travelling companion: **“Pinnalla!”**

Two passengers and one driver depart normally. Cut to the restaurant table: the
fantasy platter becomes the real illustrated meal. They do a seated shoulder
groove and share a laugh, conveyed visually. A short money beat can resolve the
₹180 meal to ₹20 remaining, if it reads cleanly without slowing the payoff.

This settlement happens between people, not inside a fabricated ORIX payment
screen. The story never announces a universal discount percentage.

### 12. The journey continues — 102–114 s

Use four short animated comic beats integrated around the table/phone, not a
grid of badges. Character hands, eyes and props still act in each shot:

- Open the real inbox/notification view: **“Updates nokkaam.”**
- Open journey history: **“Yaathrakal oridath.”**
- Complete the actual post-trip rating action: **“Yaathra engane undaayirunnu?”**
- Open support and account/privacy controls:
  **“Help veno?”** then **“Account ninte controlil.”**

Keep real in-app labels unchanged; Manglish belongs in the comic narration and
dialogue around them. Do not recolor a real app screenshot to pretend a new theme.
If individual screens need more reading time, extend this launch cut within the
proposed range rather than making unreadable one-frame feature flashes.

### 13. ORIX lands — 114–120 s

Return to the opening dance step. This time the boy and companion share the frame
and the moving route line brings in the original ORIX mark. The mandhi character
does one last small dance beside the scene, not over the call to action.

**ORIX**

**“Ore vazhi. Koode pokaam.”**

**“Ippo journey plan cheyyaam.”**

**orixrideshare.tech**

Hold the final lockup and URL still enough to read while the character settles.
End with the chosen musical phrase. No unrelated crash, sonic logo or voice tag.

## Animation and art direction

Original comic styling informed by colorful superhero-comic edit grammar: heavy
ink contours, halftone shadows, hatching, bold graphic color blocks, stepped
character poses, fast smear drawings and perspective-driven action. Kerala and
ORIX supply the specific world. No franchise characters or borrowed logos.

- Boy: one consistent face/outfit/bag across turns, dance/walk poses, phone reads,
  delighted/puzzled/hopeful reactions and a seated payoff. Head leads a take;
  shoulders, arms and bag follow on offset curves. Feet contact the ground.
- Driver: seated idle groove, steering-wheel finger tap, lean, question, pointing
  and two-finger fare gesture. His body is not one flat bouncing layer.
- Helper/companion: distinct silhouettes and individual timing; not synchronized
  clones. The helper has a separate destination, resolving the story logic.
- Food: a custom platter spin, cooked drumstick kick and rice-wave cycle. Stage
  these as acting assets with several poses, not one illustration bobbing in y.
- Environment: SJCET facade geometry, palms, bag/cloth follow-through, parked-auto
  details, moving road layers and restrained steam. Parallax is supporting motion.
- Transitions: platter-to-wheel match cut; thought bubble expands into fantasy;
  route stroke joins scenes; a character's hand/phone motivates a panel wipe.
- Use vivid yellow, cyan/turquoise, vermilion, green and dark ink. Actual product
  views retain their existing monochrome pixel UI and original logo.
- Use imagegen for coherent character/pose designs, settings and textured art;
  use code for rigs, timing, bubbles, typography, transforms and editable effects.
  Plan mouth/eye/arm variants and layer separation before generating final assets.

Proposed output is the existing landscape default until the owner chooses the
delivery format. The mention of reels is a style reference, not a confirmed aspect
ratio. Keep essential acting central for a later portrait version if requested.
No final production assets are generated as part of this script-research task.

## Manglish editorial check

Manglish has variable informal spellings; these are consistent, pronunciation-led
editorial choices, not a claim of a single official Roman spelling standard.
Colloquial speech is intentional. The owner can tune local SJCET/Pala slang at
approval. Malayalam forms below check the intended meaning; they are not intended
as extra on-screen subtitles.

| Final spelling | Malayalam/meaning | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SJCET Palai | St. Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology, Palai | Correct college, not similarly named St. John's institutions |
| Bharananganam | ഭരണങ്ങാനം | Corrects “bahraninganam”; a destination near Pala, not another spelling of Pala |
| Pala / Palai | പാലാ | Use Palai in the institution's name; Pala for conversational regional reference |
| mandhi | മന്തി; food | Keep owner's familiar spelling consistently; English menus also use “Mandi” |
| Chetta | ചേട്ടാ | Familiar respectful address to an older male |
| Enthu patti? | എന്തു പറ്റി? | “What happened?”; corrects “enthu patti” capitalization/punctuation |
| kazhikkaam | കഴിക്കാം | “Let's eat”; retain zh and doubled kk |
| koottukaare | കൂട്ടുകാരെ | Friends, as the object of “call” |
| cheythaal | ചെയ്താൽ | “If [you] do”; suitable in the sharing line |
| thikayilla | തികയില്ല | “Will not be enough” |
| yaathra | യാത്ര | Journey/travel; use the same aa spelling throughout |
| orumichu / koode | ഒരുമിച്ച് / കൂടെ | Together; “koode” is the shorter final-tagline choice |
| Pinnalla! | colloquial agreement | An enthusiastic “Of course!”; not a literal translated slogan |

Lettering stays editable and authored after artwork generation. Avoid generated
image lettering, excessive all-caps, moving text during reading or paragraph-size
bubbles. Native product labels remain as captured. The international-typography
skill was consulted for mixed-language lettering considerations.
